About

Thoughtful design, with a touch of creativity.

I specialize in transforming ideas into beautifully designed experiences. From statement balloon installations, custom backdrops, and eye-catching centerpieces to full event details, I focus on creating celebrations that feel personal, polished, and unforgettable.

Whether you’re planning an intimate celebration or a large-scale event, I’m here to bring your vision together with thoughtful design, attention to detail, and a touch of creativity. From décor to entertainment, including DJs and photo booths, I can help create an experience your guests will remember long after the celebration ends.

What I create

Décor, details, and the full experience.

  1. 01

    Statement balloon installations

    Organic garlands, columns, and sculptural pieces designed as the focal point of your celebration — scaled for intimate rooms or grand venues.

  2. 02

    Custom backdrops & signs

  3. 03

    Full event décor

    Centerpieces, dessert tables, carts, and the finishing details that make a space feel complete — cohesive from the first impression to the last.

  4. 04

    Entertainment

    DJs and photo booths coordinated with the décor, so the atmosphere, music, and memories all belong to the same celebration.
